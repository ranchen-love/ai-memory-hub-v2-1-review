# Life archive taxonomy

The registry defines twelve categories, 52 subdomains and 134 categorized field slots.
The same field slot can apply to several subdomains, and is counted once within its category.
Use the [machine-readable field registry](field-registry.json) for field IDs, types, source guidance, timing and sensitivity.

The blank profile starts with every category unknown. A recorded field keeps the source and evidence status of the entry.
Use explicit date precision and validity intervals. Add a successor to correct a record and keep both revisions.
