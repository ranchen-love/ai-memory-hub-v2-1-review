# Life archive taxonomy

| ID | Category | Optional extension keys |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 01 | Identity | `identity_summary`, `language`, `contact_index` |
| 02 | Childhood and development | `period`, `experience_summary`, `milestone` |
| 03 | Education and learning | `learning_stage`, `skills`, `qualification_ref` |
| 04 | Work and ventures | `role`, `project_ref`, `responsibility` |
| 05 | Personality and reflection | `trait_dimension`, `trait_description`, `self_report` |
| 06 | Relationships and community | `relationship_type`, `alias_ref`, `event_ref` |
| 07 | Health and lifestyle | `data_type_index`, `coverage_period`, `authorized_storage_ref` |
| 08 | Finances and practical conditions | `goal_direction`, `constraint`, `secure_record_ref` |
| 09 | Goals and future plans | `goal`, `time_horizon`, `project_ref` |
| 10 | AI collaboration and decisions | `capability`, `decision_ref`, `validation_at` |
| 11 | Major life events | `timeline_event_ref`, `impact`, `related_category` |
| 12 | Collection and maintenance | `coverage_gap`, `correction_ref`, `backup_index` |

A category stays unknown until reviewed evidence is entered. A user self-report retains its provenance. A hypothesis remains separate from facts. Use explicit date precision and validity intervals. To correct a record, add its successor and preserve the original with a supersession link.
